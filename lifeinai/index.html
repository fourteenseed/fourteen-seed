AI in the real world

What does AI look like in a real day’s work?

Quite a lot, as it turns out. I’m Wendy, and everything on this page is either live on the internet or quietly running my own working day.

01 / In practice

Three things that change a working day

Your inbox, triaged.

An assistant reads the overnight mail, sorts it, drafts the three replies that matter, and labels the rest. You read a summary with your coffee.

Research, in your browser.

Claude in Chrome checks a supplier, compares the menus down the road, reads the reviews you haven’t had time for, and comes back with a short note.

The admin, handed over.

An agent that can use your computer: confirmations, the spreadsheet, the form that takes twenty minutes every Monday.

You stay in charge. Nothing goes out without you seeing it, and the things that need a human still get one.
