02 / The studio

A founder’s experience.
A builder’s instinct.

technology meets people.

>>

I’ve spent 25 years growing alongside the internet, including 15 years in SaaS and product. I co-founded Anytime Booking, grew the business, and built a team before exiting operationally in 2025. That experience of both business and product shapes what I build next.

I’m an AI power user, and Fourteen Seed is where I explore what comes next. I experiment, build with coding agents, and turn promising ideas into useful software. Some become products. Some stay private. There’s room here for the idea I haven’t had yet.

Hospitality is a focus for what I’m building now. With gobblr, James brings a chef’s understanding of service, kitchens, and the people behind them. Together, we’re building around the day-to-day realities of running a food venue.
